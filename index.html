FAQ

Questions from abroad

Do you install outside Russia?

Yes. In Buratino, Kazakhstan, our crew installed the park turnkey. We can also send a supervisor to guide your local team.

Which certificates do you provide?

Every product ships with a passport. Our acrobatic tracks are certified to GOST R 56446-2015. сертификаты для ЕС, США, СНГ (EN 1176, ASTM, ЕАС)

How long does it take?

A first 2D layout takes 3–5 days, 3D about 2–3 weeks, production 3–4 months. Shipping depends on the destination. сроки доставки по регионам
